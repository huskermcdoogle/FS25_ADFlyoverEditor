1.0.3.4 - Inside sheds, floors, span picks on loops

Camera and screen
- The camera flies under roofs, so you can place points inside sheds. Settings, DISPLAY, "camera through buildings", on by default.
- Height slice: press G. The camera looks straight down and hides roofs and trees above your vehicle's height (on foot, a height you set, 4 m to start). You can still click the points under it. Also in settings, DISPLAY.
- Panels no longer show through each other. The tool card, help, menus, the warning box and the panel each have their own layer, and a click goes to the one on top.
- The tool card moves beside the help page instead of showing through it.
- The console command FlyoverResetInput no longer leaves you with no controls when you run it on foot.
- The editor wheel keeps zooming. If the game stops sending the mouse wheel to the camera zoom, the editor zooms from the wheel itself. New console command FlyoverInputDump writes the input state to the log.

Points on floors
- One ground rule for every tool. Points now land on shed floors, concrete pads and other floors just above the terrain, not on the terrain under them. Roofs, poles, fence rails and kerbs are left out. Placing, moving and grounding points use it, and so do Divide, Smooth, Straighten, the spline, road runs, the junction build and the field loop.
- Ground flags a point under a floor, even by a few centimetres. Its tolerance now goes down to 0.

Spline
- Both ends join the road more easily. The first and last point join a waypoint within about 1.5 m (further when zoomed out), lane ends first. Right-click snaps a loose last point the same way. Hold Alt for no snap.
- A last click near the start is no longer swallowed by the start point.

Span picks (Smooth, Straighten, Divide, Ground, Parallel, Merge, Move)
- Smooth's rebuild works on the stretch you picked. On a loop it could rebuild the other side.
- Picks on a loop work. Two clicks take the shorter way round and are no longer refused as "the long way round".
- Click on past the far end to carry it along the track, round a loop too. The first click stays put: click it, then where it should go, to move it.
- Every span tool acts on the span you picked, and the card's hint and NEXT line say how to change it.

Field loop
- Uses a Courseplay custom field as the boundary when the cursor is inside one. Card toggle "Courseplay field", on by default. It only shows when Courseplay is installed.
- Leaves out islands. A pylon or tree patch cut into a field no longer pulls the loop in and out of it. Card toggle "leave out islands", on by default.

Junction tool (beta)
- A fresh T (lanes stop short, no turns yet) is built in one go instead of turn by turn.
- A two-way farm road meeting a two-lane road gets its own T pattern. A one-way side road gets the same pattern, pruned.
- Junction numbers follow undo and redo, and are written only when the game saves. New console command: FlyoverForgetJunction <number>.

Help
- Help and hint text fixed to match the tools, including the new span picks.

Single player only. Requires AutoDrive (tested with 3.0.1.4).
